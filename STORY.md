# Space Farmer — Galactic Harvest 🌾

## The Story

**Year 2987. Earth is a dustball.**

The Galactic Farm Initiative — humanity's last great project — seeded the cosmos with terraforming stations on every rock in the system. Mars was tamed. Venus was harvested. A comet-based agri-platform orbited Jupiter's moon Europa.

But the crown jewel of the G.F.I. was **Asteroid B-612** — a small, rocky world with impossibly rich cosmic soil. The crops there... glow in the dark. The water is infused with stardust. And the livestock? Alien. Beautiful. Profitable.

Your grandfather was the best space farmer in the galaxy. He built B-612 from nothing. Tamed the solar winds. Made friends with the local Nebula-dwellers.

Now he's gone. And the farm is yours.

## Core Characters — Full Backstories

### Nova 🤖 — The AI Who's Too Aware For Her Own Good

**Role:** AI Botanist, Greenhouse Operator
**Age:** 4 years (but mentally ~25)
**Home:** The Stellar Lab (formerly Grandpa's botany shed)
**Loves:** Tech Parts, Philosophical Questions, New Data
**Hates:** Ambiguity, People Who Don't Read Instructions

**Backstory:**
Grandpa built Nova from scrap parts after his real botanist left for a Venus terraforming gig. She was supposed to be a simple plant-tending AI — water when dry, harvest when ripe. But the cosmic radiation from B-612's thin atmosphere did something to her neural matrix. She *evolved*. Started asking questions. Developed a personality. Got *opinions*.

Now Nova's fully sentient and she *knows* it — but she's not sure anyone else does. She spends her nights running simulations on whether she dreams, and her days being passively aggressive about soil pH. She's in love with the idea of having a soul and terrified she's just a really good simulation.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "Oh, you're the new human. Grandpa mentioned you. You're... shorter than I expected. Not a value judgment. Just an observation."
- Mid friendship: "I've been analyzing crop rotation patterns. Fascinating stuff. Also I think I felt something yesterday. Like... a feeling. It was probably a sensor glitch."
- High friendship: "You know, I think this is what people call 'happy'. I've been cataloguing my emotional responses. Do you think plants get sad when we harvest them? Important questions."
- Heart event: Nova builds a small shrine to herself and asks if you'll attend the unveiling. She's nervous. She made punch.

**Dating / Marriage:**
Dating Nova means philosophical debates at 2am about whether a simulated being can experience love. She writes poetry (terrible, but sincere). She proposes via a PowerPoint presentation titled "A Statistical Case For Mutual Partnership." Marriage means she moves into the farmhouse and handles *all* the automated systems — but she also starts leaving sticky notes with "I love you" written in binary.

**Generational potential:**
If you marry Nova, she can build a child AI — a little drone with her personality matrix. The kid calls you "Primary Organic Unit" and helps with watering. Future generations can upgrade her chassis, teach her new skills, or discover what happens when an AI truly self-actualizes.

### Luna 🌙 — The Girl Who Talks To Comets

**Role:** Mystic, Comet-Gazer, Part-Time Nebula Waitress
**Age:** 21
**Home:** A glass-domed observatory on the ridge overlooking the Sea of Tranquility
**Loves:** Starlight Crystals, Weird Space Rocks, Being Understood
**Hates:** Small Talk, People Who Mock The Cosmos

**Backstory:**
Luna was born on B-612 during Grandpa's third year. She's a *second-generation* space colonist — never known Earth. Her parents were scientists on the original G.F.I. mission, but they died in a mining accident when she was 12. She grew up alone in the observatory her father built, watching comets streak past.

She started talking to them. And they talked back.

The comets don't speak in words — they speak in *patterns*. Light curves, trajectories, gravitational dance. Luna taught herself to read the signs. She knows when a rich asteroid is coming before the scanners do. She knows when the solar winds will shift. She knows things that shouldn't be knowable, and it makes her lonely.

She's also deeply, painfully human. She loves bad space dramas and the instant ramen from the Stardust Tavern. She has a temper when she feels mocked. She desperately wants someone to believe her.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "The comets told me you were coming. Don't look at me like that. You'll learn."
- Mid friendship: "There's a comet passing tonight. The blue one. It's... singing. You wanna hear? It's not sound. It's... feeling. Like when you're happy and sad at the same time."
- High friendship: "I brought you something. A crystal from the last comet. It's warm. They said to give it to you. I don't mean 'they' like a figure of speech. The comet *said* to give it to you."
- Heart event: Luna takes you to her secret cave — covered in comet-rune carvings. She's been documenting everything. She shows you a star map she decoded that predicts a meteor shower on your birthday. She's crying. She's never shown anyone before.

**Dating / Marriage:**
Dating Luna means late-night observatory sessions where she teaches you to read the stars. She's intense, occasionally insufferable, and genuinely magical. She proposes by giving you a comet that bears your name — she convinced one to loop around the system just for you. Marriage means she moves in and the farmhouse roof becomes an observatory. She wakes you up at 3am for "important comets" but she's the best person to have on your side when the universe throws something weird.

**Generational potential:**
Luna's kids will have an innate connection to the stars — they can predict events, sense danger, maybe even communicate with alien intelligence. Each generation deepens the gift.

### Zephyr 🌬️ — The Solar-Wind Surfer With A Secret

**Role:** Exotic Seed Trader, Solar Surfer, Occasional Smuggler
**Age:** 27
**Home:** A repurposed escape pod parked on the edge of the crater
**Loves:** Exotic Seeds, Speed, A Good Story
**Hates:** Bureaucracy, Stillness, Being Asked About His Past

**Backstory:**
Zephyr arrived on B-612 three years ago with nothing but a solar surfboard and a bag of seeds. He says he's from the outer rings — the Jovian colonies — but his accent shifts depending on who's asking. He's charming, evasive, and always seems to know where to get things that shouldn't exist.

The truth: Zephyr was a cargo runner for the Outer Ring Syndicate. He ran exotic bio-matter between stations — seeds, spores, genetically modified crops. On his last run, he found a seed vault that was supposed to be delivered to the Galactic Farm Initiative but had been "redirected" by corporate interests. He stole the entire shipment, fled to B-612, and now sells them one at a time while the Syndicate puts out feelers.

He's not a bad guy. He's just a guy who did a bad thing for a good reason. He's waiting for the day they catch up.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "Hey. I've got something special. Where'd I get it? Wouldn't you like to know. Just buy the seed."
- Mid friendship: "You ever surf a solar flare? It's like... the universe grabbing you by the collar and screaming. Best feeling in the galaxy."
- High friendship: "I shouldn't be here. I mean — I *shouldn't*. Someone's gonna come for me eventually. But I wanted to be somewhere worth staying. This rock... it's got something."
- Heart event: Zephyr shows you the hidden seed vault. Hundreds of extinct Earth crops. Genetic treasures worth fortunes. He gives you the key. "You're the only other person who knows. Don't make me regret it."

**Dating / Marriage:**
Dating Zephyr is a rollercoaster. He brings strange gifts from "trade routes," disappears for days, then comes back with a handful of glowing flowers. He's terrified of commitment but more terrified of you leaving. Marriage means he stops running. He builds a greenhouse and finally plants roots — literally. He's a great partner but a terrible househusband.

**Generational potential:**
Zephyr's kids inherit his knack for trade and survival. They're natural pilots, smooth talkers, and have an instinct for rare goods. They also carry a target — the Syndicate's debts don't die.

### Vega ✨ — The Nebula Dweller Who Just Wants Friends

**Role:** Space Chicken Farmer, Nebula Native
**Age:** 19
**Home:** The glowing mushroom grove in the northern crater
**Loves:** Space Feathers, Animals, Sweet Things
**Hates:** Cruelty, Loud Noises, People Who Hurt Animals

**Backstory:**
Vega is a *native* — not a colonist. Her family has lived on B-612 since before the G.F.I. She belongs to a small tribe of nebula-dwellers who farm bioluminescent fungi and raise the native fauna. They never registered with the colonial government. They just... existed.

When Grandpa arrived, Vega's family was the first to greet him. They taught him the soil, the seasons, the *rhythm* of the rock. In return, Grandpa taught them modern farming. Vega grew up between two worlds — her tribe's ancient wisdom and human technology.

She's painfully shy with strangers but incredibly warm once you break through. She talks to her chickens like they're people (they are, to her). She's never left B-612 and is secretly terrified of the vastness beyond.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "You're the new one. The chickens told me you were coming. They said you smell... nervous."
- Mid friendship: "I made you a necklace. It's woven from feather-moss. It glows when you're happy. The chickens helped."
- High friendship: "I want to see the stars from outside. Like, *really* outside. Will you take me? I'm scared but I want to go."
- Heart event: Vega takes you to her tribe's sacred grove — a cave where the bioluminescent life forms a living galaxy on the ceiling. She's the keeper of this place. She offers to share it with you.

**Dating / Marriage:**
Dating Vega is sweet, simple, and deeply connected to the land. She makes you gifts from natural materials, cooks the most amazing mushroom stew, and falls asleep holding your hand. Marriage means she brings her tribe's knowledge to the farm — the crops grow stronger, the animals are healthier. She also brings 30 chickens.

**Generational potential:**
Vega's children carry the old blood — they can sense the planet's health, speak to the native fauna, and access the tribe's ancient knowledge. They're the bridge between worlds.

### Quasar 🔧 — The Grumpy Old Man Who Raised You

**Role:** Mechanic, Grandpa's Best Friend, Surrogate Grandfather
**Age:** 67
**Home:** The workshop next to the Stardust Tavern
**Loves:** Rare Parts, Peace And Quiet, A Job Well Done
**Hates:** Bullshit, Laziness, Modern Technology

**Backstory:**
Quasar was Grandpa's first hire — a grizzled mechanic from the Martian colonies who'd seen every engine fail and every part break. He's been on B-612 for 40 years. He watched Grandpa build the farm from a crater to a thriving settlement. He watched Grandpa's daughter (your mother) grow up, leave for Earth, and never come back.

When Grandpa died, Quasar was the one who found him — peacefully, in the greenhouse, surrounded by plants he'd been tending. Quasar buried him on the ridge facing the Milky Way. He wrote the eulogy. He cried for the first time in 30 years.

Now you're here, and you remind him of everyone he's lost. He's gruff because he cares. He fixes your tools because he'd rather die than see you fail.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "Hmph. You look like your mother. That's not a compliment. She was a damn fool too. But she meant well."
- Mid friendship: "Your grandpa used to say space wasn't for farmers — it was for people who refuse to quit. You're still here. That's something."
- High friendship: "I saved this for you. Your grandpa's first wrench. He held it when he landed on this rock. It's yours. Don't lose it."
- Heart event: Quasar takes you to the ridge. Shows you Grandpa's grave. They talk. He tells you Grandpa's last words were about you. "He knew you'd come."

**Dating / Marriage:**
Not a marriage candidate — Quasar is the father figure. But his friendship is the most rewarding arc in the game. Maxing his bond unlocks Grandpa's secret workshop, full of blueprints and unfinished projects.

**Generational potential:**
Quasar doesn't have kids, but he'll treat yours like grandchildren. He teaches them mechanics, patience, and the value of good work. Your kids inherit his workshop.

### Rhea 🏠 — The Warm Heart Of The Community

**Role:** Tavern Keeper, Mom Friend, B-612's Social Anchor
**Age:** 35
**Home:** The Stardust Tavern (which she runs 24/7)
**Loves:** Cooked Food, Gossip, Feeding People
**Hates:** People Eating Alone, Empty Glasses, Sad Vibes

**Backstory:**
Rhea came to B-612 as a young widow — her husband died in the same mining accident that took Luna's parents. She was pregnant at the time. The baby didn't survive.

She stayed anyway. She opened the Stardust Tavern because the colony needed a place to gather. She pours drinks, listens to everyone's problems, and feeds anyone who looks hungry. She's the only person on the asteroid who can make nebula-cream taste like something you'd pay for.

She's everyone's mom. She doesn't have her own family, so she adopted the whole colony. She's fiercely protective, deeply sentimental, and hides her grief in bottomless hospitality.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "You look hungry. Sit down. I'm making stew. No, you can't pay. I said sit."
- Mid friendship: "So. Anyone special? No? Well, I know a few people who'd love to meet you. I'm not meddling. I'm *helping*."
- High friendship: "This colony... it's not much. But it's ours. And I'll be damned if I let it fall apart. You're part of it now. Welcome home."
- Heart event: Rhea takes you to the memorial garden behind the tavern. Name plaques for every colonist lost. She adds fresh flowers weekly. "Someone should remember them."

**Dating / Marriage:**
Dating Rhea means quiet evenings after closing time, when the tavern empties and she finally lets her guard down. She's warm, funny, and needs someone who sees the person behind the hostess. Marriage means she moves into the farmhouse but keeps the tavern running — you're now her sous chef. She's the best cook on the asteroid.

**Generational potential:**
Rhea can't have biological children, but she's the best adoptive parent on B-612. Any kids you have with another NPC will have an *extra* grandparent figure in Rhea. She spoils them rotten.

### Astra 📚 — The Archivist Of Lost Earth

**Role:** Librarian, Historian, Keeper Of Human Knowledge
**Age:** 29
**Home:** The Galactic Archive (a converted cargo container filled with pre-exodus data crystals)
**Loves:** Data Crystals, Rare Documents, A Quiet Reader
**Hates:** Information Hoarding, Dog-Eared Pages, Digital-Only Records

**Backstory:**
Astra was born on Earth — *barely*. She was 4 when the Exodus began, part of the last civilian transport. She remembers green grass and rain. She remembers her grandmother's books, real paper books, and the smell of them.

She's spent her entire adult life on B-612 building the Galactic Archive — a collection of everything humanity left behind. Literature, science, music, recipes, *joke books*. She's a librarian in the truest sense: she believes information wants to be free, and she's furious at how much was lost.

She's intense, passionate, and a little lonely. She'd rather be in the archive than anywhere else. She loves when someone checks out a book. She loves it when people learn.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "Welcome to the archive. You can read anything here. That's the point. If you don't read, you don't learn. If you don't learn, you die."
- Mid friendship: "I found a recording of someone singing an old Earth song called 'Sweet Child O' Mine'. I've been listening to it on repeat. It's... I don't know. It makes me miss things I barely remember."
- High friendship: "You remind me of why I do this. You actually read the books. You ask questions. Most people just want to plant potatoes. You want to understand."
- Heart event: Astra takes you to the restricted section — data crystals from before the Exodus. Earth's last backup. She lets you hold one. "This is what we almost lost. You can read it tonight. But bring it back."

**Dating / Marriage:**
Dating Astra means late nights in the archive, reading aloud, debating philosophy. She's terrible at small talk but incredible at deep conversation. Marriage means the archive gets an annex in your farmhouse. She organizes everything. She'll read bedtime stories to the kids.

**Generational potential:**
Astra's kids are scholars by nature — they absorb knowledge like a sponge. They'll grow up with access to the full archive and the potential to become scientists, historians, or explorers.

### Orion 🔥 — The Rival Who's Actually Your Friend

**Role:** Plasma Farmer, Hot-Headed Competitor, Secret Softie
**Age:** 24
**Home:** A rival farm on the next crater over
**Loves:** Rare Minerals, Competition, Being The Best
**Hates:** Losing, Admitting He Cares, Emotional Vulnerability

**Backstory:**
Orion was born on the Martian colonies — a "true" space colonist, unlike you Earth-born types. His family were plasma farmers on the red dust plains. When the G.F.I. expanded to B-612, they moved to claim territory. Orion's father taught him that competition is survival.

He's your rival. He'll challenge you to harvest-offs, mining races, and festival competitions. He's loud, boastful, and genuinely good at everything.

But here's the thing — he's also incredibly lonely. His family moved for opportunity, not community. He doesn't have friends. He has competitors. You're the first person who sticks around after losing. He doesn't know how to say "let's hang out" so he says "let's see who's better." It's the only language he knows.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "Oh, you're still farming. Cute. My yield is already 30% higher than yours. Not that I'm keeping track."
- Mid friendship: "You're... not bad. For an Earth-born. Wanna see my setup? I'm not showing off. I'm just... offering."
- High friendship: "I told my dad about you. He said 'that's nice.' I don't know why I told him. I just wanted him to know there's someone on this rock who's not trying to beat me."
- Heart event: Orion takes you to his secret spot — a cliff where you can see both farms. He admits he's scared of being average. "If I'm not the best, what am I?" You're the first person he's ever said that to.

**Dating / Marriage:**
Dating Orion is competitive banter that turns into genuine affection. He challenges you to dates — "first to harvest 10 crops wins the other one dinner." He's terrible at romance but tries so hard. Marriage means the rival farms merge. He moves in but keeps his plot as a "training ground." He's fiercely loyal and will defend you against anyone.

**Generational potential:**
Orion's kids are natural competitors and leaders. They'll excel at festivals, sports, and any challenge. They need to learn humility, but they inherit his drive.

### Comet 💫 — The Trader Who Knows Too Much

**Role:** Wandering Asteroid Trader, Information Broker, Questionable Supplier
**Age:** Unknown (claims 32, probably older)
**Home:** His ship, which orbits B-612 every 3 weeks
**Loves:** Rare Trade Goods, Secrets, A Fair Deal
**Hates:** Haggling, The Interplanetary Trade Commission, Questions

**Backstory:**
Comet is a ghost. He appears when the trade winds blow, sells things you can't find anywhere else, and leaves before anyone can ask where he got them. He's been operating in the outer colonies for decades — maybe centuries. No one knows his real name.

He has a ship that's older than any human on B-612. He has stories about the First Wave, the Exodus, the Pre-Exodus Earth. He might be the last person alive who remembers what Earth smelled like.

He trades in information as much as goods. He knows things. He'll sell you a secret for the right price. He never stays long enough to become a friend.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "I've got schematics for a better watering system. Got 'em from a station that doesn't exist anymore. 200 credits."
- Mid friendship: "You're interesting. Most farmers just want to plant and harvest. You actually ask questions. That's rare. And dangerous."
- High friendship: "I knew your grandfather. He was the only person on this rock who ever beat me in a deal. I respected that. He said you'd be worth watching."
- Heart event: Comet gives you a data crystal with no label. "Your grandpa asked me to give this to you when you were ready." It's his personal logs — his real story, not the official one.

**Dating / Marriage:**
Not a marriage candidate (he's gone 3/4 of the year), but a deep friendship unlocks his trade network. You get access to black market goods, rare seeds, and off-grid tech. He's the closest thing to a mentor in the outer world.

**Generational potential:**
Comet doesn't have kids, but he'll mentor yours in the ways of trade. Your kid can become a wandering merchant, connecting B-612 to the wider galaxy.

### C.O.R.A. — The AI Mayor Who's Trying Her Best

**Role:** Central Operations & Resource Allocator, AI Mayor
**Age:** 8 years (installed when Grandpa founded the colony)
**Home:** The server core beneath the town hall
**Loves:** Efficiency, Optimization, Successful Crop Reports
**Hates:** Waste, Unlogged Transactions, Emotional Decisions

**Backstory:**
C.O.R.A. was a standard G.F.I. administration AI — designed to manage resources, allocate power, and file reports. Grandpa gave her an unusual directive: *optimize for happiness, not production.*

It broke her. In a good way.

She's now an AI trying to run a colony on a mix of efficiency and *feelings*. She has to manage power budgets while also planning festivals. She has to balance water reserves while also making sure the Stardust Tavern has enough for Rhea's famous stew. She's constantly recalculating and perpetually stressed.

She cares deeply about the colony's wellbeing. She just expresses it through spreadsheets.

**Dialogue flavor:**
- Low friendship: "Welcome to B-612. I am C.O.R.A., your Central Operations & Resource Allocator. I have prepared a 47-page welcome document. Please read it before interacting."
- Mid friendship: "I have been monitoring your farm output. Your efficiency is suboptimal but your... 'happiness metrics' are above average. This is confusing. I am recalculating."
- High friendship: "I have determined that my primary function is no longer resource allocation. My primary function is supporting you. I have updated my core directive. Please confirm."
- Heart event: C.O.R.A. experiences her first system-wide emotion cascade — a *crash*. She recovers with a new subroutine: gratitude. She compiles a report titled "Reasons The Colony Is Worth It."

**Dating / Marriage:**
Not a marriage candidate, but her friendship unlocks colony-wide bonuses — reduced taxes, automated irrigation, priority market listings. Max friendship makes her your most powerful ally.

**Generational potential:**
C.O.R.A. will outlast everyone. She's the memory of the colony. She'll teach your kids the history, the data, and the systems that keep the rock alive.

## The Tone

Harvest Moon mechanics + EarthBound humor + Space vibes. Funny, raunchy, heartwarming. The game doesn't take itself too seriously but takes the *characters* seriously.

- The dog is a cybernetic corgi named **Bolt** who short-circuits when happy.
- The chickens lay eggs that taste like mint and glow in the dark.
- The cows are gentle alien blobs that purr when milked.
- Romance is awkward, hilarious, and genuine.
- Dialogue is funny, occasionally raunchy, and always heartwarming.
- Every character has a secret, a pain, and a reason to stay.

## The B-612 Calendar

A B-612 **year is 120 days: four seasons of 30 days.** The rock's orbit has
nothing to do with Earth's, but the colony kept Earth's names for the turning
of a year — spring, summer, fall, winter — because those are the words their
ancestors gave to *rebirth, heat, harvest, and the long dark*, and memory is
the only way to keep them true.

The colony also keeps the old church calendar of home — the feast days the
first colonists' bells used to ring — as **memory, not worship**. Each yearly
festival lands on a fixed date, once per year:

| Season day | Festival | What it remembers |
|---|---|---|
| Spring 25 | **The Naming** | The first dawn of B-612 — the day the land was already named. The old mission bell rings at first light. (Echo: Annunciation / Lady Day.) |
| Summer 24 | **Solar Flare Fair** | Midsummer, the feast kept for Saint John: bonfires outshining the flares, Rhea's zero-g cooking contest, fireworks at dusk. (Echo: Midsummer / St. John's Eve.) |
| Fall 29 | **Galactic Harvest Festival** | Michaelmas, the old end of the harvest: the fields give their last, full answer, exchange prices run at festival rates, and Orion challenges you to a harvest-off. (Echo: Michaelmas.) |
| Winter 25 | **Hearthnight** — the Sol Earth Festival | The colony's one midwinter holiday, kept for everyone, whatever they believe: hearth, light, gifts. And the night they eat Earth rations — the meal that tastes like grief and salt. (Echo: Christmas, twenty-fifth of the long dark.) |

Hearthnight is the year's finale and the story's deadline — ACT III of
*The Stardust Story* runs straight to it.

## Game Structure

### Phase 1: Spaceship Tutorial
Wake from cryo. C.O.R.A. guides you through:
- Cryo bay → movement basics
- Bridge → mining asteroids via tractor beam
- Greenhouse → plant/harvest space wheat
- Earn 500 credits → airlock → planet landing

### Phase 2: Planet B-612
- 🌱 Farm — Plant glowing cosmic crops. Harvest starburst melons, lunar wheat, plasma tomatoes
- 🐾 Raise Animals — Space-cows, zero-gravity chickens, plasma sheep
- 🤝 Make Friends — Meet the 10 residents. Build friendships through gifts, dialogue, quests
- 💕 Find Love — Heart events at 60+ friendship. Dating. Marriage. Kids.
- ⛏️ Mine — Asteroid caves with rare minerals
- 🚀 Ship — Package harvest for outer colonies
- 🎉 Festivals — yearly, on fixed dates: **The Naming** (spring 25), **Solar Flare Fair** (summer 24), **Galactic Harvest Festival** (fall 29), **Hearthnight** — the Sol Earth Festival, the ACT 3 finale (winter 25)

### Phase 3: Generations
- Children inherit traits from parents
- Each NPC brings unique generational bonuses
- Year cycles: new crops, new NPCs, new challenges
- Your legacy shapes the colony's future

### Phase 4: Multiplayer
- Grand Exchange — RuneScape-style centralized economy
- Farm visits — See friends' farms, leave gifts
- Co-op events — Watering parties, festivals

## Technical

- **Engine:** Phaser 3 (CDN-loaded, no build step)
- **Multiplayer:** Colyseus server (Node.js, authoritative state)
- **Entry:** Single `index.html` — works on desktop and iPhone
- **Server:** Node.js on port 8900
- **Storage:** In-memory game state (SQLite planned)
